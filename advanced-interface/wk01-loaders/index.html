Serene / Blue loaders

Three concepts, each with an indeterminate and a determinate loader, built on the Serene / Blue design system. Every loader moves through Zero, Progress, and Complete. Complete is shown by light, with an ice-white glow.

Concept 01

Capsule

Pills with dark knobs. In the indeterminate loader, the knobs glide back and forth over 9.6s, each a third of a cycle apart, so they never all move together. In the determinate loader, one knob moves along the track and the gradient fill follows it.

Concept 02

Petal flip motion

Six petals, each turning on its own long axis like a coin. Progress is shown by the turning itself, not by color or brightness.

In the indeterminate loader, every petal is always turning, each a sixth of a turn behind its neighbour, so the movement never stops. In the determinate loader, the petals start edge-on and turn to face you in overlapping sequence as progress fills.

Concept 03

Pool

Concentric rounded squares, like ripples in still water. Each layer is a radial gradient set to Screen, so where the layers overlap they add up to a white glow at the center. In the indeterminate loader, the ripples swell outward from the center. In the determinate loader, the rings fill in from the center out, and all four layers glow together at Complete.
